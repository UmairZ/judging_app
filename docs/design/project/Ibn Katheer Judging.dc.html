2026 Ibn Katheer Qur'an Competition

Judging System — Visual Design Language & Screens

A live, offline-capable judging app for a Qur'an memorization contest. Two roles — judges grading contestants in real time, and one admin running the leaderboard and configuration. The live grading screen leads; everything is tuned for speed, calm, and zero-hesitation input.

Live Grading — the centerpiece

Interactive — tap + and −. Running score, auto-flag DQ & voice all live.
A calm cream surface with a green header. Each deduction has its own stepper — tap + to log, − to correct a mis-tap (no separate undo). Every key carries a plain-language definition, the count is always visible, and the session score moves instantly — never a spinner. When a question's hifz bottoms out, the “Call it?” auto-flag appears.
